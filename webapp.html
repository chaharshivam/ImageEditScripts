Framewipe

Prep frames locally. Nothing uploaded.

A local tool on this Mac. Strip metadata, split 2x2 grids into Instagram-ready 4:5 and 9:16 frames, and clean videos. Files never leave the machine.

Choose what to do

Nothing but metadata changes. No splitting, cropping, resizing or re-encoding. JPEG, PNG and WebP are rewritten losslessly, so the pixels come out bit-identical to what you put in.
Finds the gutters by looking at the pixels rather than assuming exact quarters, then crops and upscales each panel to exact Instagram dimensions. Use mirror when the same filename appears in more than one input folder.
Strips all container metadata and re-encodes to Instagram dimensions. The default crops nothing away.
Looks for something small, bright and motionless in each corner. It cannot tell a watermark from a poster, caption or ceiling light — you pick the right one from the results.
Safer than painting. Uses the engine --suggest-crop to find the largest 4:5 and 9:16 crops that leave the mark out of frame. Run Find watermark first if you do not have a box.
Run Find watermark first and pick a box from its results. A wrong box will erase the wrong part of your video. Prefer Crop watermark out when the mark sits in a corner.
